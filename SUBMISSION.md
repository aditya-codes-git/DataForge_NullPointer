## Quick Description

SaySure is an evidence-driven voice-quality engineering layer that sits between application content and Text-to-Speech. It detects speech-risk tokens (technical terms, currency, identifiers, versions, acronyms), generates controlled pronunciation candidates, synthesizes both raw and controlled text concurrently through Rime TTS, and provides side-by-side auditory comparison so engineers can verify exactly what listeners will hear — with full provenance, human verification, and reproducible evidence for every decision.
